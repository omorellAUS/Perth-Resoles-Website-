How to order

  1. Scan the QR code on the Perth Resoles board at Urban Jungle Climbing Gym (or keep going here).
  2. Select your options: rubber, rand repair, shoe model, shoe size and your contact details.
  3. Pay securely with Square. Your shoe details and order number are attached to your order automatically.
  4. Tag your shoes with your name and order number and hand them in at the Urban Jungle counter. Tags are on the little wooden Perth Resoles board at the gym.

Turnaround is 3 weeks from when we collect your shoes.
